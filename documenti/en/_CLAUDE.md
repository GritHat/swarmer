## Agent swarm

This project has a swarm of specialist agents in `sciame/`: professionals and sub-professionals, agents
`sciame-<sigla>`. It is convened only when the user asks, with a strategy: **singolo** (`/sciame-singolo`),
**ventaglio** (`/sciame-ventaglio`), **classica** (`/sciame-classica`), **giuria** (`/sciame-giuria`), **giuria leggera** (`/sciame-giuria-leggera`), **completa**
(`/sciame-completa`), **plenaria** (`/sciame-plenaria`), **new expert** (`/sciame-esperto`) or **auto** (`/sciame`).
The rules are in `sciame/PREAMBOLO.md`, the strategies in `sciame/PIPELINE.md`, jury and plenary in
`sciame/GIURIA.md`, the cards in `sciame/AGENTI.md`; the local cards in `sciame/locale/` take priority.
