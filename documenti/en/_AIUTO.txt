
COMMANDS (bash sciame/sciame.sh <command>)
  installa (install) [--lingua L] [--istruzioni] [--forza] [--cartella DIR] [--locale DIR] [--gestiti-dal-progetto]
  aggiorna (update)            like installa, with the files of this sciame.sh; files modified in the project stay,
                               the new version goes alongside them with the .nuovo suffix
  singolo (single) | ventaglio (fan-out) | classica (classic) | giuria (jury) | giuria-leggera (light-jury) | completa (full) | plenaria (plenary)
      | esperto (expert) | auto  "request"
      [--profilo P] [--ruoli a,b] [--lingua L] [--da-file FILE] [--modello M] [--stampa] [--mostra] [-- claude options]
  lingua (language) [L]        the swarm's language (agents, skills, documents); without L it shows it
  ruoli (roles) ["question"]   the roles and profiles; with a question, those its words call up
  professionisti (professionals)  the tree of professionals and sub-professionals
  controlla (check) | rigenera (regenerate) | rimuovi (remove) | istruzioni (instructions) [--togli] | prove (tests)
  modelli (models) [ruolo=modello …]  the model of a role, of a core, or of all (--tutti=modello)
  richieste (requests)         the missing skills that have reached the creation threshold
  conta (count) CARTELLA GIRO  the tally of a jury
  approva (approve) FILE.json  adds an approved professional: database, agents, skills and sciame.sh
  proponi (propose) SIGLA… [--invia]  the pull request of the approved entries to the public repository (UPSTREAM)
  importa-schede (import-cards) AGENTI.md  brings into the database the cards of a 3.x AGENTI.md
  verifica (verify) | costruisci (build)  the checks before publishing; the rebuild of sciame.sh

The project's local cards live in sciame/locale/ and always take priority over the generic roles.
